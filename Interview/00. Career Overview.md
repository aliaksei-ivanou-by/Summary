## 03 Career Shape in One Paragraph

The progression runs from measurement and field engineering, through algorithm research and development, into commercial software. The Land Cadastre Information Centre was two years of hardware in the field and diagnosis across independent failure domains. PELENG was more than seven years of image-processing algorithms for flown satellite instruments, where the binding constraints were an onboard computation budget and the fact that nothing can be patched after launch. RIFTEK was the first commercial C++ role and the bridge between the algorithm work and the software career that followed. The independent period was where that algorithm background became production-oriented C++ practice. EPAM was enterprise delivery - modern C++17 on one project, a decades-old C89 platform on the other - with a promotion and, mid-project, a relocation to Warsaw that did not interrupt the work. Innowise is embedded product work: the same discipline about irreversible decisions and layered systems, now applied to a physical device, and a technical-lead role alongside hands-on delivery as the engineering sub-group grew from four to six.

## 04 Cross-Cutting Themes

Four themes recur across periods. Reach for the matching one when a job description names it.

**Design under hard constraints.** Onboard compression at PELENG, chosen from a computationally cheap algorithm family because the transform-based alternative did not fit the flight hardware. On the SIP platform, Yocto and the inherited Qt Widgets foundation were kept because a reproducible layered firmware image mattered more than a cleaner starting point.

**Diagnosis across independent layers.** Vehicle monitoring, where one symptom on the operator's map had to be traced across four independent domains - satellite, installation, cellular network and server. Sierra, where a Java or Scala symptom led back to a C root cause. The SIP platform, where a fault could sit in the application, the SDK, provisioning, firmware or hardware.

**Working safely in inherited code.** C89 at Sierra, legacy C++ in the oil-and-gas system, an inherited Qt call flow and a .NET provisioning tool at Innowise - in each case the work was to change behavior inside code that was not going to be rewritten.

**Making behavior measurable.** Automated resolution assessment at PELENG, which replaced expert manual analysis of flight-test imagery with a repeatable number. Stage-by-stage inspection in the RIFTEK pipeline, so a bad mesh could be attributed to the stage that caused it. Focused Qt Test suites and repeatable on-device scenarios on the SIP platform, where hardware-dependent behavior could only be confirmed on a real phone.
