## 03 Career Shape in One Paragraph

The line runs from measurement and field engineering, through algorithm Research & Development, into commercial software. The Land Cadastre Information Centre was hardware in the field and diagnosis across independent failure domains. PELENG was seven and a half years of image-processing algorithms for flown satellite instruments, where the binding constraints were an onboard computation budget and the fact that nothing can be patched after launch. RIFTEK was the first commercial C++ role and the bridge between the algorithm work and the software career that followed. The independent period converted that algorithm background into production-oriented C++ practice. EPAM was enterprise delivery - modern C++17 on one project, a decades-old C89 platform on the other - with a promotion and a relocation along the way. Innowise is embedded product work, where the same discipline about irreversible decisions and layered systems applies to a physical device, and where a lead component was added to individual delivery as the engineering sub-group grew from four to six.

## 04 Cross-Cutting Themes

Four themes recur across periods and are worth reaching for when a job description names one of them.

**Design under hard constraints.** Onboard compression at PELENG, chosen from a computationally cheap algorithm family because the better one did not fit the hardware. The same reasoning on the embedded SIP platform.

**Diagnosis across independent layers.** Vehicle monitoring from a symptom on a map; Sierra from a Java or Scala symptom into a C root cause; the SIP platform across application, SDK, provisioning, firmware and hardware.

**Working safely in inherited code.** C89 at Sierra, legacy C++ in the oil-and-gas system, an inherited Qt call flow and .NET tool at Innowise.

**Making behavior measurable.** Automated resolution assessment at PELENG, stage-by-stage inspection in the RIFTEK pipeline, QtTest coverage on the embedded target.
