## Highlights

Worked in the research department of the Space Research and Design Division on image-processing algorithms and supporting software for Earth remote-sensing satellite payloads and a UAV optical system.

• Designed and implemented MATLAB algorithms for satellite frame stitching, radiometric matching, onboard compression, adaptive quantisation, and image-quality assessment.
• Developed an ADPCM-family lossy compression algorithm achieving a 3-4x ratio within a constrained onboard computation budget, followed by image-type prediction and adaptive quantisation to improve quality at the same ratio.
• Built a frame-stitching algorithm that joined segments from a multi-detector focal plane into continuous imagery using geometric alignment and radiometric matching.
• Automated linear-resolution assessment of flight-test imagery, replacing manual expert analysis with repeatable, operator-independent measurement.
• Developed UAV processing modules for debayering, stabilisation, image fusion, and format conversion; calibrated IMU-to-camera alignment for frame-centre georeferencing.
• Designed a remote-sensing satellite reference database and web interface using Ruby on Rails and PostgreSQL.
• Operated the optical payload during 30+ UAV test flights and participated in satellite flight tests and in-orbit operation control.
• Authored a short analytical paper on integrated space monitoring as an evolution of GIS, combining Earth observation with GNSS positioning and small-satellite constellations.
