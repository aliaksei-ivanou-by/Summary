## Highlights

At Innowise, I combine hands-on product development with technical-lead responsibilities. My main project assignment from May 2025 to August 2026 was an embedded SIP/IP desk-phone platform for a network-connectivity equipment manufacturer.

• Developed and debugged C++17/Qt 5.15 functionality for ARM64 devices running Yocto-based Embedded Linux, covering asynchronous call and account state, contacts, BLF, audio/video calls, conferencing, network and certificate settings, and hardware integration.
• Implemented a registration watchdog and primary/backup SIP-server failover and failback in a customised Linphone SDK, then integrated its APIs across the Qt application, diagnostics, settings, and provisioning flows.
• Refactored a stateful call subsystem towards explicit sessions, services, and Linphone adapters.
• Modernised a Windows Autoprovisioning Manager from .NET Framework 4.8 to .NET 8 and developed device discovery, configuration generation, DHCP/TFTP delivery, SIP Digest realm probing, encrypted project persistence, and related workflows.
• Contributed to Yocto recipes, image packaging, service startup, and cross-layer debugging on physical ARM64 phones.
• Worked on phone-side WebRTC/mediasoup integration and made a limited contribution to the initial Node.js conferencing server.
• Took technical-lead responsibility for an internal engineering subgroup that grew from four to six engineers, covering architectural guidance, code review, task planning, onboarding, mentoring, performance evaluation, and interview support while remaining roughly 80% hands-on.
