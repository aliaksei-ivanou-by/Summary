## Highlights

Worked in the Department of Navigation and Information Technologies, deploying and supporting GPS/GLONASS vehicle-monitoring systems for fleet operators.

• Installed and connected approximately 200 monitoring terminals across more than 10 cities on roughly 200 road and rail vehicles.
• Configured onboard terminals and the server side of the monitoring platform for new vehicles and clients.
• Diagnosed faults across GNSS reception, vehicle electrics, terminal hardware, cellular connectivity, and server-side configuration.
• Handled equipment acceptance and repair and coordinated procurement with manufacturers to support deployment and maintenance work.
• Provided client support and training so operators could use the monitoring system independently and report faults accurately.
• Developed the organisation's web presence using Joomla and Drupal.

This was primarily a field and integration engineering role rather than a software-development position.
