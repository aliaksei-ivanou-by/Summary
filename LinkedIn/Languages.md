# LinkedIn Languages

> Purpose: exact values to enter in the LinkedIn Languages section. The proficiency level describes current practical ability, not the highest completed course module.

## Entries

| Language | Proficiency level |
|---|---|
| English | Professional working proficiency |
| Polish | Elementary proficiency |
| Swedish | Elementary proficiency |
| Russian | Native or bilingual proficiency |
| Belarusian | Native or bilingual proficiency |

## Notes

- **English:** current level B2 and the professional working language used at EPAM Systems and Innowise Group.
- **Polish:** current self-assessed level A2. The completed B1.1 and B1.2 modules document study progression but are not a state B1 certificate or a claim of current B1 proficiency. Keep the course sequence in [Education](<./Education/2022.09-2024.08 - Accent School of Polish - Polish.md>).
- **Swedish:** current self-assessed level A2 after lack of regular practice. The courses completed through B2 remain recorded in [Education](<./Education/2022.01-2023.06 - Centre for Swedish Studies - Swedish.md>).
- **Russian and Belarusian:** native languages.

## Do Not Select

- Do not select **Limited working proficiency** for Polish solely because the B1.1 and B1.2 course modules were completed.
- Do not select **Full professional proficiency** for English unless the current self-assessment changes from B2 to C1 or equivalent professional fluency.
