# Sorbent Systems for Water Monitoring and Plant Materials

## Project name

Sorbent Systems for Water Monitoring and Plant Materials

## Description

School research conducted weekly at the Department of Analytical Chemistry, Belarusian State University, under university supervision.

• Surveyed water and bottom sediments at five points along the Svislach River in Minsk and two reference locations.
• Measured pH, total salt concentration, chloride content, hardness, buffering capacity, redox properties, and the ability of sediments to bind heavy metals using ionometry, potentiometric titration, and frontal chromatography.
• Investigated roots, leaves, and seed coats as renewable cation-exchange materials, deriving titration curves and Bronsted pK spectra under NaCl and CuCl2 electrolyte backgrounds.
• Used reference sources measured with the same methods to make the river results interpretable without an external comparison dataset.
• Produced three archived research texts, including an English version, and contributed to conference publications and presentations at district, city, university, national, and international levels.

The work found substantial variation in total salt concentration between sampling points, strong heavy-metal binding by bottom sediments, and high ion-exchange capacity in several plant materials.
