# GPS and GLONASS Vehicle Monitoring Systems

## Project name

GPS and GLONASS Vehicle Monitoring Systems

## Description

Deployment and support of satellite vehicle-monitoring systems for fleet operators across Belarus.

• Installed and connected approximately 200 GPS/GLONASS tracking terminals across more than 10 cities on roughly 200 road and rail vehicles.
• Configured onboard equipment and the server side of the monitoring platform for new vehicles and clients.
• Diagnosed failures across GNSS reception, antenna placement, vehicle electrics, terminal hardware, cellular telemetry, and server configuration.
• Performed routine maintenance, equipment acceptance, and repair, and coordinated procurement with equipment manufacturers.
• Trained and supported client operators and developed the organisation's web presence using Joomla and Drupal.

The central engineering challenge was tracing one visible symptom across independent physical, radio, network, and server domains while minimising travel to geographically distributed deployments.
