# Transient Luminous Events in the Upper Atmosphere

## Project name

Transient Luminous Events in the Upper Atmosphere

## Description

Third-year radiophysics coursework, graded 9/10, examining sprites, jets, and related electrical discharges and flashes above thunderstorms.

• Produced a 26-page research review from primary scientific literature.
• Reconstructed a coherent physical account from sources covering different parts of the phenomenon.
• Connected the observational record to atmospheric structure, electric fields, ion formation, and the physics of upper-atmospheric discharges.
• Reviewed the discovery of transient luminous events and the observational and experimental campaigns used to investigate them.

The result was a defended coursework paper presenting the physical mechanism and observational evidence as one consistent account.

The recorded source identifies this as third-year coursework completed in 2010; the September 2009 to June 2010 dates represent the corresponding academic year because exact project months were not recorded separately.
