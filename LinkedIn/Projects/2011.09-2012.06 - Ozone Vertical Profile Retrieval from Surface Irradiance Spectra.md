# Ozone Vertical Profile Retrieval from Surface Irradiance Spectra

## Project name

Ozone Vertical Profile Retrieval from Surface Irradiance Spectra

## Description

Diploma thesis, graded 9/10, investigating whether atmospheric ozone vertical profiles could be retrieved from spectral irradiance measured at the surface.

• Extended the standard Aladin ozone-profile parameterisation so it could represent profiles characteristic of anomalous regions.
• Developed a MATLAB program that generated vertical ozone profiles from the extended model.
• Analysed the influence of model parameters on the resulting profiles.
• Built an atmosphere model and used libRadtran to calculate direct, downward-scattered, and upward-scattered surface irradiance spectra under different parameterisations.
• Identified target functions required for the inverse retrieval method.

The 49-page thesis contained 38 figures, 3 tables, and 56 sources. It demonstrated a realistic route to the retrieval method and was completed within the state programme for monitoring the Earth's polar regions and supporting Arctic and Antarctic expeditions.

The September 2011 to June 2012 dates represent the fifth academic year and thesis period.
