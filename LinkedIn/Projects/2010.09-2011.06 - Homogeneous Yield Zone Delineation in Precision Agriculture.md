# Homogeneous Yield Zone Delineation in Precision Agriculture

## Project name

Homogeneous Yield Zone Delineation in Precision Agriculture

## Description

Fourth-year radiophysics coursework, graded 9/10, on partitioning agricultural fields into statistically homogeneous yield zones.

• Developed a statistical bioequivalence method for comparing the average yields of field plots using Monte Carlo simulation from fitted normal distributions.
• Formulated a zoning method based on expectation-maximisation over a Gaussian mixture model.
• Implemented the EM iteration, distance calculation, and supporting workflow in MATLAB.
• Built a synthetic-data test harness with known cluster parameters and verified that the implementation recovered the planted structure.
• Addressed the normality and independence assumptions by sampling spatially separated plots rather than treating neighbouring measurements as independent.

The defended 39-page study produced two justified algorithms, a working and validated clustering implementation, and an explicit account of the method's limitations.

The source identifies this as fourth-year coursework completed in 2011; the dates represent the corresponding academic year because exact project months were not recorded separately.
