## Description

Completed the English-language International Summer Space School “Advanced Technologies for Nanosatellite Experiments in Space,” worth 3.5 ECTS.

The programme covered:

• Piggyback nanosatellite launch.
• Microsatellite design using Altium Designer.
• Solar and near-Earth space physics.
• Onboard electronic and microprocessor systems.
• Control and communication systems.
• Orientation and attitude stabilisation.
• Space navigation and motion determination.
• Tether-system dynamics.
• Low-orbit motion and de-orbiting.

Laboratory work included PCB development, navigation, solar and Earth physics, tether systems, de-orbiting, and onboard electronics.
