## Description

Completed a five-year Specialist Degree in Radiophysics comprising 5,874 academic hours, with a specialization in Satellite Information Systems and Technologies.

• Diploma thesis, 9/10: investigated atmospheric ozone-profile retrieval from surface spectral irradiance measurements; extended the Aladin parameterization model, developed a MATLAB profile-generation program, and performed radiative-transfer modelling with libRadtran.
• Implemented and validated EM/Gaussian-mixture clustering in MATLAB for precision-agriculture yield zoning; 9/10.
• Completed a research review of transient luminous events; 9/10.
• Completed a 540-hour pre-diploma research placement; 9/10.

Academic subjects included radiophysics, electronics, signal processing, remote sensing, satellite navigation, GIS, statistics, atmospheric physics, and scientific computing.
