## Description

Completed the English-language International Summer Space School “Advanced Technologies for Nanosatellite Experiments in Space,” worth 3.5 ECTS.

The programme covered:

• Space systems engineering and standards.
• Flight dynamics and attitude control.
• GNSS fundamentals and hardware.
• Mission and system requirements.
• Functional architecture and operational modes.
• Link- and power-budget calculations.
• Advanced space technologies.

Laboratory work included satellite design in Pro/ENGINEER, requirements development, link- and power-budget calculations, and practical NOAA satellite reception.
